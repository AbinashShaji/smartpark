Availability Forecasting

Know before you go.

Current availability only tells you half the story. If a lot is empty now, will it still be empty when you arrive in an hour?

SmartPark utilizes historical occupancy data and machine learning models to forecast future availability. By analyzing past trends, the system provides a clear prediction of how busy a location will be at your specific planned arrival time.

This empowers you to make informed decisions—whether that means leaving earlier, choosing a different location, or reserving immediately before the lot fills up.

Reservation Engine

Parking that fits your vehicle.

Not all parking slots are created equal, and neither are vehicles. SmartPark's reservation engine requires your vehicle type and size category before confirming any booking.

This allows the system to instantly filter out physically incompatible slots. If you drive a large SUV, you will never be assigned a compact car space.

Once you select a compatible slot, the system immediately marks it as reserved across the entire network, absolutely preventing any conflicting bookings or double-booking scenarios.
