Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about finding, reserving, and accessing parking with SmartPark.

Getting Started

SmartPark is a centralized digital platform that helps drivers find available parking, choose a slot that fits their specific vehicle size, reserve it in advance, and seamlessly access the parking facility without physical tickets.

SmartPark is available to any driver looking for managed parking spaces in our network. You simply need to create an account, register your vehicle details, and you can start reserving slots immediately.

Finding & Reserving Parking

Log in to your SmartPark dashboard to access the location directory. The platform displays real-time capacity and availability data for all integrated parking facilities in the network.

Once you select a location, the system will display the current availability. You can view the exact number of open spots before committing to a reservation.

Parking Access

Upon completing a reservation, you will receive digital access credentials in your account dashboard. You present these credentials at the facility's entry terminal to gain automated access.

The dynamic QR code is your primary digital key. You simply scan the code from your smartphone screen at the facility's entry scanner to lift the barrier.

The One-Time Passcode (OTP) serves as an alternative access method. If you cannot scan a QR code, you can manually enter this secure PIN on the entry terminal's keypad.

Because your reservation and payment were handled digitally in advance, exit is seamless. You do not need to stop at a payment machine or deal with paper tickets on your way out.

Payments

SmartPark operates a fully integrated digital payment system. You pay securely during the reservation checkout process using standard payment methods, locking in your slot and your rate simultaneously.

Once payment is confirmed, your slot state is updated to "Reserved," and the system generates your access credentials (QR Code and OTP). The booking is permanently recorded in your account history.

SmartPark Technology

We utilize historical occupancy data combined with machine learning models to analyze patterns in parking usage over time. This allows the platform to forecast how busy a location is likely to be when you plan to arrive.

The prediction system acts as a smart guide to help you decide when and where to park. By offering a data-driven forecast of expected capacity, it helps reduce the uncertainty of arriving at a potentially full facility.
