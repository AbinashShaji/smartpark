Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SmartPark ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our centralized parking reservation platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide deterministic parking reservations and manage our facilities:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and secure password hashes for authentication.
  • Vehicle Information: Vehicle type and category, collected specifically during the booking process to ensure slot compatibility. (Note: We do not collect vehicle numbers during registration).
  • Booking Data: Reservation times, selected locations, and payment records.
  • Access Logs: Timestamps of entry and exit events triggered via QR code or OTP credentials to manage slot states.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To facilitate parking searches and validate compatible slots.
  • To process reservations and payments securely.
  • To generate secure QR codes and OTPs for frictionless access.
  • To train our Machine Learning models (Linear Regression) on historical occupancy data to provide availability forecasting. This data is aggregated and anonymized.

3. Data Security

SmartPark implements robust security measures including secure sessions, protected routes, and database abstraction to protect your data. All passwords are cryptographically hashed, and OTP/QR credentials are validated securely server-side.
